EasyList and EasyPrivacy
========================

Map Browser's built-in content blocker uses two filter lists by
The EasyList authors (https://easylist.to/):

  EasyList      https://easylist.to/easylist/easylist.txt
  EasyPrivacy   https://easylist.to/easylist/easyprivacy.txt

They ship unmodified as resources/filters/easylist.txt and
resources/filters/easyprivacy.txt in the source repository, and as
Contents/Resources/filters/ in the app bundle.
